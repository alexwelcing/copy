## About Lupine

Lupine builds a unified computational platform for materials science research. It replaces fragmented legacy toolchains (VASP, LAMMPS, custom scripts) with a single Rust codebase integrating quantum DFT calculations, machine-learned interatomic potentials, and molecular dynamics simulations.

### Products

**Lupine View** (Shipping Now)
- WebGPU-powered molecular visualization
- Drag-and-drop LAMMPS file parsing in browser
- 10M+ atoms at 60fps rendering
- Publication-quality 3D in two seconds
- Free, no licensing required
- Replaces paid tools like OVITO Pro ($4,500/license)

**Lupine Distill** (Research Intelligence)
- Autonomous discovery engine analyzing published MD research
- Recovers canonical physics laws from simulation data
- 15 canonical laws recovered, 49 passing tests, 47 theorems formally proven in Lean 4
- Validates against ground truth before discovering novel relationships

### Technical Stack
- Rust (memory-safe, GPU acceleration)
- WebGPU (browser-native GPU compute)
- WASM (near-native speed, zero install friction)
- Lean 4 (formal verification of algorithms)
- Apache 2.0 (open source core)

## Market Context

### The Problem
- Researchers stitch together 4-6 disconnected tools for a single workflow
- VASP dominates but is proprietary and expensive ($4,500/license)
- LAMMPS dates to 1995 with legacy architecture
- Manual validation and metadata loss across pipeline stages
- No unified platform exists

### Why Now (Three Tailwinds)
1. ML potentials (MACE, NequIP) proven at DFT-accuracy with MD speed
2. WebGPU enables browser-native GPU compute without installation barriers
3. National sovereignty concerns regarding Austrian-held VASP infrastructure

### Addressable Market
- 50,000+ LAMMPS users globally
- Scientific computing/CAE market growing to $20B+ by 2028
- Nations seeking sovereign computational infrastructure
- Industries: battery development, semiconductors, aerospace alloys, nuclear materials

## Target Audience Segments

### Segment 1: Computational Researchers (Primary)
- **Who:** PhD students, postdocs, and PIs running MD/DFT simulations daily
- **Pain:** Tool fragmentation, license costs, reproducibility friction
- **Current state:** Stitching VASP + LAMMPS + OVITO + custom scripts
- **Decision driver:** "Does it actually work with my workflow?"

### Segment 2: Lab PIs / Research Directors
- **Who:** Decision-makers who approve tool adoption and allocate compute budgets
- **Pain:** License costs, onboarding new students, reproducibility of published results
- **Decision driver:** Cost savings, student productivity, publication quality

### Segment 3: National Labs / Sovereign Compute Buyers
- **Who:** DOE labs, defense agencies, national research institutions
- **Pain:** Dependence on foreign-held infrastructure (VASP/Austria)
- **Decision driver:** Sovereignty, security, open-source auditability

### Segment 4: Industry R&D (Battery, Semiconductor, Aerospace)
- **Who:** Materials engineers at companies developing advanced materials
- **Pain:** Slow simulation cycles, expensive toolchains, siloed teams
- **Decision driver:** Speed to results, cost, collaboration
