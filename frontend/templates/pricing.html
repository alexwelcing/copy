Questions

Frequently Asked

What are tokens?

Tokens are the unit of AI processing. A typical campaign brief uses 2,000–5,000 tokens. The Starter plan's 1M tokens covers roughly 200–500 campaign executions per month.

What happens when I hit my token limit?

On Starter, execution pauses until your next billing cycle. On Growth and Enterprise, you keep working and overage is billed at your plan's rate—no interruptions.

Which agents do I get on Starter?

Director, Copywriter, and Editor—the core execution team. Upgrade to Growth to unlock the Strategist, Optimizer, and Analyst for full-stack campaign intelligence.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated billing.

What counts as an "agent run"?

Each time you spawn an agent to execute a skill—writing copy, auditing a page, building an email sequence—that's one run. Concurrent agents let you run multiple in parallel.
