New Skill March 12, 2026

Creative Strategist

Diagnose and fix the #1 reason ads underperform: state mismatch between your creative and the viewer's mindset at the moment of impression.

The Problem

~90% of ads are built for pain-state viewers — people actively feeling a problem. But most ad impressions land on prospective-state viewers — people thinking about the problem while scrolling a feed.

This mismatch is invisible in most creative reviews, but it's the single biggest source of wasted ad spend. Meta's Andromeda system evaluates creative first and matches it to behavioral signals, which means pain-state creative gets unstable delivery by design.

Two States of Mind

Pain-State

"I'm so tired of this. Nothing works."

Feeling the problem right now. Reactive, urgent.

Prospective-State

"I keep imagining what it would look like if..."

Thinking about the problem. Reflective, curious.

The 20-Minute Diagnostic

The skill includes a complete diagnostic framework you can run on any ad account in under 20 minutes.

STEP 01

Pull the Metrics

Hook Rate and Hold Rate, side by side, for every active creative.

STEP 02

Read the Pattern

High Hook + Low Hold? Your body pivots to features too fast. Stay in the movie.

STEP 03

Retention Curves

Sharp drop at 3-6s? State mismatch. Gradual decline? Wrong register. Drop at end? CTA state break.

STEP 04

Pre-Flight Check

Three questions before anything goes live: state, register, and close.

THE BOTTOM LINE

The ad creative winning right now is prospective — it sounds like someone thinking out loud, not pitching. Pain-based concepts are unstable in the ad account because delivery systems match creative to organic behavioral signals, and organic behavior is overwhelmingly reflective, not reactive.
